# References

Research and inspiration. Nothing here is a project decision by itself.

- Gosling, Rentfrow & Swann (2003), *A very brief measure of the Big-Five personality domains* (TIPI).
- Aron et al. (1997), *The Experimental Generation of Interpersonal Closeness* (the 36 questions).
- Granovetter (1973), *The Strength of Weak Ties* (friends of friends).
- Johnson & Goldstein (2003), *Do Defaults Save Lives?* (defaults decide outcomes).
- Park et al. (2023), *Generative Agents: Interactive Simulacra of Human Behavior*; a16z AI Town. Ideas only, no code.
- Games for pacing and relationships: Stardew Valley (schedules, friendship over repeated meetings),
  Animal Crossing (villagers remember you), The Sims (personality drives initiative), Travel Frog (come back to postcards).
- Push/pull framework (team whiteboard): pulls are disclosure, similarity, familiarity, reciprocity,
  belonging; pushes are effort, judgment, difference, uncertainty, obligation, rejection.
