# Temporary guest residents and explicit demo pacing

Status: Proposed; implemented under the user request on 2026-10-25. Team ratification
and numbering remain for main. Does not amend principles or decision 0004.

Guests should not leave permanent live residents behind. Track actual server connections
and verified SpacetimeAuth email ownership privately. Retire guest residents 60 seconds
after their last connection closes; a short reconnect cancels expiry. Verified account
residents persist offline. Keep private historical records reversibly instead of deleting
ambiguous historical owners. Claiming a guest through the existing capability transfers
ownership and restores only residents auto-retired by this policy. Old unknown identities
are not retroactively labelled from names. Explicit guest exit forgets its browser token.

Demo control belongs to a world host or a publisher-designated operator. Show an explicit
ON/OFF switch and a read-only indicator for other members. Opening it routes the host and
one nearby real resident to a common destination without teleportation. It shortens dwell
to eight seconds and bypasses ordinary conversational cooldown/new-material pacing only
while active. One encounter at a time in that world and at least 45 seconds between starts
bounds model work. Automatically turn off after five minutes. Ordinary mode retains its
previous pacing; the evidence judge, consent, source privacy and good-fit limits are unchanged.
