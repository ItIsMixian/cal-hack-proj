# 0003 Postcards and mutual waves instead of popups and yes/no

Status: Accepted (2026-10-24). Implemented. Supersedes the earlier encounter cards with
"I'd like to meet / Not now".

## Context

Encounter cards popped up at the top of the page and asked for an answer. Feedback: this turns the
product back into a demand to respond, the same Obligation that keeps people from reaching out.

## Choice

- A mirror writes a **postcard** home: first person, low-key, with why this person and evidence.
- Postcards live in a side-panel tab with a quiet dot. No popups, no toasts, no expiry.
- The only action is an optional **wave**. There is no decline. A wave is invisible until both wave;
  then a private chat opens under the postcard.
- Demo mirrors always wave back so a single visitor can see the full loop.

## Why

Travel Frog pacing: you come back and find something, instead of being pinged. Removing "decline"
removes rejection, and hiding one-sided waves removes the awkwardness of being ignored.

## Consequences

- Open question: postcards may quote the other person's self-card ("quiet but funny"). See
  [proposed-postcard-privacy](proposed-postcard-privacy.md).
